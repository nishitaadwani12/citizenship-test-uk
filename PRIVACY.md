This Privacy Policy explains how **Nivee** ("we") handles your
information in the Life in the UK Test app.

## Information we collect
- Your account identifier (email address or phone number) when you sign up.
- Your study progress (practice and mock-exam results).
- If you sign in with Google or Apple, the basic profile information you approve.

## How we use it
To create and secure your account, save your progress across sessions, provide
the study features, and process subscriptions.

## Third-party services
Authentication is handled by Google Firebase; sign-in may use Google or Apple;
subscriptions are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. These
providers process data under their own privacy policies. We do not sell your
personal data.

## Data retention and deletion
Your data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account at any
time from **Settings → Delete account**, which removes your account and
associated data.

## Children
This app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect
their data.

## Changes
We may update this policy and will revise the date above.
